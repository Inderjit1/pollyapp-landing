Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. Polly stores all of your data locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. Nothing is sent to a server, and there is no account or sign-in required. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I cancel or manage my Polly+ subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple. On your iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions, select Polly, and change or cancel your plan there. Cancelling doesn't delete any of your data.

How do I export my data for a doctor's appointment?

Open the app, go to Settings, and use the Export Reports feature to generate a PDF summary of your cycle history, symptoms, and lab results.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

Email michaelsingh790@gmail.com with as much detail as you can (what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead) and we'll take a look.

Does Polly provide medical advice?

No. Polly is a personal tracking tool. Reference ranges shown for lab values are general guidelines from public medical sources, not a diagnosis. Always discuss your results with a qualified healthcare provider.
