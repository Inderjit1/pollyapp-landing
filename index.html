Privacy

Your health data isn't a product.

Health data is among the most sensitive information a person generates. Polly was built from the ground up so that nothing you enter ever leaves your phone.

📱

On-device only

All data is stored in Apple's SwiftData framework on your iPhone. Nothing is transmitted to any server — because there is no server.

🔐

No account required

Polly never asks for your email, name, or any identifying information. You open the app and start tracking. That's it.

🗑

Delete is delete

When you delete Polly, your data is gone. There's no backup copy in a database somewhere. There's no "30-day hold" period. It's your phone.

🔍

The technical proof: Polly has no network entitlements and no API calls to external servers. You can verify this yourself with a packet sniffer — Polly makes zero outbound network requests with your health data. The App Store receipt check for subscriptions goes to Apple directly, not to us.
